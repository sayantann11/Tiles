The problems we fix

Eight things that cost a tile shop money every month

The problemWhat TileDesk does
1Udhaar keeps growingContractors and builders take goods on credit. Nobody knows who is 90 days late until cash runs short.Credit list with ageing (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days), credit limit per party, one-tap WhatsApp reminder, full ledger.
2Shade / batch mismatch complaintsA second lot from another batch looks different on the floor. The customer blames the shop.Stock is kept batch by batch with shade. The bill warns when an order is bigger than one batch, before the goods leave.
3Box vs sq ft confusionCustomer gives area, shop sells boxes. Wrong maths means extra trips, returns and lost margin.Enter room area and wastage %. Boxes, pieces, sq ft and price are worked out instantly for every tile size.
4Stock is a guessShowroom plus two godowns, broken boxes, dead designs. Owner must phone the godown to answer a customer.Live stock per location in boxes and sq ft, low-stock alerts, damaged-box write-off that also records the loss.
5Slow, handwritten quotesCustomer visits three shops. The one that sends a clear quote first usually wins.Room-by-room quotation in about a minute, sent on WhatsApp, converted to a bill in one tap.
6Leads are forgottenArchitects, walk-ins, Instagram and IndiaMART enquiries sit in a diary and are never followed up.Lead board (New → Site visit → Quoted → Won) with follow-up dates and a "call today" list on the dashboard.
7Hidden costs eat the marginHamali, tempo, mistri commission and breakage are small daily cash payments nobody adds up.Five-second expense entry by category, expense as % of sales, and estimated profit for the month.
8The owner must be in the shopWithout the owner, nobody can say today's sales or who paid.Everything works on the phone: today's sales, credit, stock and follow-ups wherever you are.
